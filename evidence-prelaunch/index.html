P01 · One repeated team moment: what does it teach?

Loading…Pending approval

Carousel · Proposed 2026-10-06 · Week 1

Download six PNGsLinkedIn PDFCover

Vaney LinkedIn

A meeting opening, a handoff, a response when someone asks for help. Choose one moment your team repeats. Observe it first. Culture is whatever an environment produces, whether we intended it or not. That's one of the starting ideas in the practice I'm developing. Write down what happened in that moment. Leave judgment out. Consider what the response might teach folks to expect next time. Then choose one response to make on purpose. Write down why. Try it when the moment comes around again. Look back afterward. Record what happened before deciding what to repeat or adjust. P10 · Culture is what the environment produces

Loading…Pending approval

Carousel · Proposed 2026-10-15 · Week 2

Download six PNGsLinkedIn PDFCover

Vaney LinkedIn

Culture is whatever your environment produces. That includes what you intended. It also includes what you didn't. A value statement can tell us what an organization wants. It can't tell us everything about what happens inside it. We have to observe that. Look at how folks respond when someone disagrees. Look at what happens when somebody asks for help. Pay attention to what people learn to keep to themselves. That's part of the culture. We can spend a lot of time describing the behavior we want. Then we get frustrated when people behave differently. But the conditions around them belong in that conversation too. We created the conditions, but we punished the outcomes. Design means making decisions about what something should do. Culture asks us to bring that same intention to the environment people work in. It is already producing something. Start by observing what that is. Use the Culture Scorecard to choose where to start: https://hello.letsthink3d.com/culture-scorecard
